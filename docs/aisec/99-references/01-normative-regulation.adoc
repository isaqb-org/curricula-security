=== Normative (Regulation)

* [EU AI Act] Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence. Official Journal of the European Union. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng

* [GDPR] Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Official Journal of the European Union.

* [CRA] Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act). Vulnerability-handling and reporting obligations apply from 11 September 2026; remaining obligations from 11 December 2027.

* [NIS2] Directive (EU) 2022/2555.
